Tierra's Movie Hit List

My Top Five Movies of all-time.

All about the benjamins

I love to laugh & I like the chemistry between Ice Cube & Mike Epps guaranteed to make you laugh and feel good.

Next Friday

I love this movie because it's hillarious and it has Ice Cube, Mike Epps, and a host of other funny comedians. It's a feel good movie & will have you laughing from begining to end. The best one of the Series.

Menance II Society

Great action packed, rugged film. Depicts the life of growing up in south central L.A. through the eyes of an African American teen.

Love Jones

This is one of the greatest love stories ever told. You will laugh, cry, smile, & overall feel great while watching this movie made in Chicago.

Love & Basketball

Story of childhood friends who became lovers and never let the love die, passionate love story.
